## A desktop model for teaching

Our team is building a compact teaching model for younger students. It brings suspension and drivetrain components together so students can see how the parts fit and move.

## Completed CAD

I designed the wishbone suspension from scratch to fit the desktop assembly and modelled a custom universal joint to go with it.

## Differential in development

I am developing the differential for the same assembly. The suspension and universal joint are complete at the CAD stage.
